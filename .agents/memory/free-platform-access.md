---
name: Free platform access
description: Product rule separating free Masakhe platform access from customer payments collected by businesses.
---

## The rule
All core Masakhe platform features are available without a subscription, trial, or platform payment. Payments a business collects from its own customers are separate and must continue working.

**Why:** The user directed that people should be able to use Masakhe without paying and that platform access should be free.

**How to apply:** Do not reintroduce module paywalls, Masakhe subscription checkout, trial expiry, or user-facing platform pricing. Preserve invoice and payment features businesses use with their customers.
